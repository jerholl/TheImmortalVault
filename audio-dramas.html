Horror Audio Drama (Series)  ·  Supernatural Horror / Mystery

CULBURN (Working Title)

When a cynical foster kid travels to a town that doesn't appear on any map to claim his estranged mother's estate, he discovers he's been lured into a predatory sanctuary run by ancient vampires who feed on the life force of their "devout" congregation.

Milo Evans, a young man who grew up in the foster system and remembers little of his birth mother, is contacted by a mysterious lawyer regarding her estate in the remote town of Culburn. Upon arrival, he finds a community where the local gas station attendant has never heard of the town he's standing in, and the town itself seems to exist in a warped reality.

As Milo attempts to settle his mother's affairs, he is relentlessly pressured into attending the local church by a cast of "friendly" but unsettling neighbors. He soon realizes the town is a feeding ground: the charismatic Reverend Parker is a psychic vampire who drains the life force of his patrons, while the seemingly sweet diner owner, Georgina Sweets, is a blood-crazed vampire who maintains her youth by feeding on "misbehaved" children. Trapped by a local mechanic who sabotages his car to prevent escape, Milo must navigate a web of ancient predators and childhood trauma to uncover the truth about his mother's "madness" and his own forgotten past before he becomes the next permanent resident of Culburn.
